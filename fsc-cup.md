## Game format

- 2 x 15-minute halves.
- 2-minute half-time interval.
- Size 3 football.
- 4 v 4 with no goalkeeper.
- Kick-ins from sidelines.
- 3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss.

## Expectations

This is a mixed U6/U7 competition, so some opponents may be older or more experienced than what our players are used to. The focus for our team is effort, listening, teamwork, and enjoying the day.

We have 7 players in the squad, so there will be lots of rotations throughout the day.

## What to bring and know

- Players should wear the usual Warilla kit with red tops.
- Football boots and shin pads must be worn by all players.
- Spectators are not allowed on the field. The grandstands will be open for spectators.
- No food is to be consumed on the synthetic field.
- The canteen will be open with hot coffee, cold drinks, hot chips, a sausage sizzle, and snacks available for purchase.
- Bring a drink bottle, warm clothing, and easy snacks for between games, such as fruit, orange slices, bananas, or small quick-energy snacks if needed. Please keep food away from the synthetic field.
